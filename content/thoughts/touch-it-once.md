## The Observation

Touch it once. When something lands, finish it or triage it on the first touch. Do not pick it up, set it down half-handled, and pay the full re-entry cost later. Touching without deciding is how piles become careers.

I learned that young. At sixteen, through business class and Business Professionals of America, I interviewed for an On the Job Training slot and got hired as a teller at Norwest Bank, the company that became Wells Fargo. Stillwater in May 1993. Through March 1998 I moved from that floor to the Norwest Center downtown Minneapolis (now Wells Fargo Center), then across the street to IDS as lead teller, then to sales and service supervisor with a second drive-up branch on Portland Avenue while they found a new supervisor. Somewhere in that stretch, leadership training put the line in my mouth: touch it once.

On the bank floor, that meant the piece of paper and the customer problem. Touch the form once: file it, send it, or put it in one real queue with a next action. Later it meant the inbox: open it and decide, or name who owns it and when. I didn't invent the phrase. I just never stopped using it.

I think the reason it lasted is simple. Decades later it still explains why some weeks stay light and some weeks drown in the same unfinished objects.

## Connections

This is the personal root of a line that already shows up in other cards as systems language. In [Principles Over Predictions](/thoughts/principles-over-predictions/) I wrote "touch it once, make it findable by any system" as a structured-data principle, not a forecast. Same bone, different altitude. On the bank floor it was paper and people. In the vault and the platform it means capture once and make it searchable so nobody has to re-live the decision.

It sits next to Priority Was Singular. If everything stays open, nothing is first. Touch-it-once is how priority stays operational instead of decorative. It also fights the default in Deciding vs. Defaulting: re-touching feels like work while it delays the actual choice. Busyness inside half-handled items is a convincing disguise for not deciding.

Physician Heal Thyself is the triage cousin. Only-you, with-guardrails, automatic. Touch-it-once is the moment of sort. The later framework is how you stop being the only person who can finish the touch.

On the data side, Two Trust Systems uses the same words for catalog quality: one human touch that agents can trust. Origin story first. Platform language second. Same rule.
